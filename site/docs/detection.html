What it detects

Every rule PhishGuard uses, how many points it adds and the exact words you see. This page is generated from the engine's own rule table, so it always matches the app.

How a fake site is spotted

Spelling comparisons either miss fakes or flag real sites: HDFC and HSBC, booking.com and cooking.com look alike on paper. PhishGuard asks a better question, following the research systems Phishpedia (USENIX Security 2021) and PhishIntention (USENIX Security 2022): which brand does this page claim to be, and is it on that brand's real website?

  • 107 brands: Indian banks and payment apps, government services and global brands, each with its real domains and 286 favicon fingerprints in all.
  • The claimed brand is read from the page's copied favicon, logo, site name and title.
  • India's .bank.in and .gov.in zones, which only verified owners can register, are understood.
  • Free hosting (blogspot, vercel.app, pages.dev, firebaseapp and others) is never trusted as a brand: much of today's phishing lives there.
  • News sites and forums writing about a bank are not impersonations.

Scoring

Points add up within each group and are capped at 100. Danger starts at 50, Caution at 20. A rule marked adds to others only strengthens a warning another rule started; shown, not scored appears in the privacy report without affecting the verdict. Words in italics are filled in with the details of the link.

AI Guard

AI Guard is a small model (1.3 MB) trained for this project. It finds hidden text written to trick AI assistants, known as prompt injection. On its test set it scores F1 0.915 and takes about 3 ms per text.
