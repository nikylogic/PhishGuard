How it works

Between the click and the browser.

PhishGuard becomes your default browser, so links from other apps come to it first. Links you click inside your browser stay there.

01 /

You click a link

Trusted sites open in your real browser at once. Everything else, and anything that is not a plain web link (javascript:, ms-msdt:), is held back.

02 /

The sandbox opens it

A separate Chrome or Edge with a fresh profile: no cookies, no passwords, no files. A network gatekeeper keeps it away from your router and your PC. Downloads go to quarantine and never run.

03 /

You see the verdict

Clear Caution Danger, a screenshot and every reason in plain words. Open it, open a clean copy without tracking tags, keep it locked or block the site.

Detection

Independent checks. Any one can raise the alarm.

Fake logins

Brand on the wrong domain

For 107 banks, payment apps and brands, a page that looks like the brand but is not on the brand's own domain is stopped, however the address is spelled.

Downloads

Files judged by contents

Every file is caught in quarantine and identified by what is inside, so invoice.pdf.exe is exposed as a program. Nothing is ever run.

Scams

Traps and fake alerts

Fake virus alerts, full-screen and keyboard traps, "ClickFix" copy-this-command pages, crypto-miners, and links that try to launch Windows tools.

Privacy

Trackers and AI tricks

A privacy report of trackers and fingerprinting. AI Guard, a model trained for this project, finds hidden text written to fool AI assistants.

HDFC is never mistaken for HSBC.

Spelling comparisons either miss fakes or flag real sites. PhishGuard asks a better question, following the research systems Phishpedia and PhishIntention: which brand does this page claim to be, and is it on that brand's real website?

  • Copied favicons, logos, site names and titles reveal the claimed brand
  • Understands India's verified .bank.in and .gov.in zones
  • Free hosting (vercel.app, pages.dev, blogspot…) is never trusted as a brand
  • About 380,000 reported phishing domains, checked on your PC, refreshed every 6 hours
  • Brand-new domains are flagged through RDAP and certificate history

Privacy and safety

Your links never leave your PC.

  • Everything runs locally. History, screenshots and settings stay in your own Windows user folder.
  • Phishing lists are downloaded and checked on your PC; your links are never sent to a lookup service.
  • Website age lookups send only the domain name, never the full link.
  • The AI second opinion is off by default, and when on it can only raise the risk, never lower it.
  • Hide my IP (optional) checks sites through Tor, Cloudflare WARP or your own proxy.
  • Signed automatic updates. An update installs only if it carries PhishGuard's digital signature.

Install

Two minutes, no admin rights.

01 /

Download

Get PhishGuard-Setup.exe from the latest release and run it.

02 /

Make it your default

Open PhishGuard, then Settings → General → Make PhishGuard my default browser. Windows asks you to confirm.

03 /

Pick your real browser

Chrome, Edge, Brave or Firefox. Safe links open there, directly.

04 /

Forget about it

PhishGuard starts quietly with Windows, sits next to the clock, and updates itself.

Windows SmartScreen may say the installer is from an unknown publisher, because it is not code-signed. Choose More info → Run anyway, or build it yourself from the source code. Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge must be installed (Windows 11 includes Edge).

Questions

Good to know.

Something else? Ask on GitHub.

Is it really free?

Yes, for personal use and for noncommercial organisations such as schools, charities and public bodies, under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0. Using it in or for a business needs a commercial license. The full source code is on GitHub.

Does it replace my antivirus?

No. Antivirus protects files and programs on your PC. PhishGuard protects the moment you click a link from another app, before the page reaches your real browser. They work well together.

Will it slow me down?

Sites on your trusted list open in about a quarter of a second. A full check of an unknown site, with the screenshot, usually takes two to three seconds, and you can trust a site with one click.

Which links does it check?

Links you click in other apps: messengers, e-mail programs, PDFs, documents, games. Links you click inside your browser stay in your browser; you can paste any link into PhishGuard to check it too.

Can several people use it on one PC?

Yes. Choose Several people on first start: each person signs in, and admins and analysts get different rights. Every change is written to an audit log.

How do updates work?

PhishGuard looks for a new release twice a day, downloads it, checks its digital signature and fingerprint, and installs it silently when no link is being checked. Your history and settings are kept. You can choose to be asked first, or turn updates off.
