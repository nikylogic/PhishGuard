How a fake site is spotted

Spelling comparisons either miss fakes or flag real sites: HDFC and HSBC, booking.com and cooking.com look alike on paper. PhishGuard asks a better question, following the research systems Phishpedia (USENIX Security 2021) and PhishIntention (USENIX Security 2022): which brand does this page claim to be, and is it on that brand's real website?

  • {{brand_count}} brands: Indian banks and payment apps, government services and global brands, each with its real domains and {{favicon_count}} favicon fingerprints in all.
  • The claimed brand is read from the page's copied favicon, logo, site name and title.
  • India's .bank.in and .gov.in zones, which only verified owners can register, are understood.
  • Free hosting (blogspot, vercel.app, pages.dev, firebaseapp and others) is never trusted as a brand: much of today's phishing lives there.
  • News sites and forums writing about a bank are not impersonations.
