# Code of Conduct

PhishGuard exists to protect people, many of whom are not technical and some of whom have already lost money to scams. We expect the same care in how we treat each other.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people new to security or to GitHub.
- Assume good faith. Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Help people who report scams. Never mock someone for having been fooled.
- Keep discussions on topic and in plain language.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Posting live, clickable phishing or malware links. Defang them: `hxxps://example[.]com`.
- Using PhishGuard's issues, data or code to build or improve scams.
- Sexual content, threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/nikylogic). Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block people who break these rules, temporarily or permanently, depending on how serious it is.

This code applies in all PhishGuard spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone represents the project.
